# Dentora entity copy

Use these descriptions consistently in legitimate company profiles, partner pages, interviews, and directory listings. Keep links pointed at the matching French or Arabic page, and only add claims that can be supported by the current product.

## Short description

**Français**

Dentora est une plateforme marocaine qui aide les patients à trouver des dentistes et des cliniques dentaires, et accompagne les praticiens avec Dental App, un logiciel de gestion de cabinet dentaire.

**العربية**

Dentora منصة مغربية تساعد المرضى على العثور على أطباء وعيادات الأسنان، وتوفر للممارسين Dental App، برنامجاً لإدارة عيادات الأسنان.

## Partner link guidance

When a participating clinic links to Dentora, prefer its own clinic profile URL over the general homepage. Use natural link text such as the clinic name or “fiche de la clinique sur Dentora”; avoid repeating exact-match keyword anchors across partners. Where appropriate, link from the clinic's own site to its profile and from its profile back to its official site.

For editorial mentions, describe both sides of the platform accurately: the patient directory and Dental App for dental practices. Do not describe Dentora as a dental association, certification authority, or medical provider.
